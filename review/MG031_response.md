All five items are accepted. Item 4 identifies an error of mine: draft 1 stated that fractional Fourier shifts
round-trip at round-off. They do not. Measured on the actual start field, the errors are 7.81e-6 (h/4, 3h/4) and
1.56e-5 (h/2), matching ChatGPT's table. The cause is the nonzero Nyquist coefficient on the even grid.
