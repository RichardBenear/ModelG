MG036a / MG8 Draft 2 is substantially improved, but I recommend one more revision before registration. The original drift error is corrected, and the conclusions are now appropriately limited. Remaining issues concern the definition of the deterministic experiment, convergence scoring, and validation.
I reviewed [Draft 2](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/3f88b8562f430419ca9a4c50731b28dd4063b74a/spec/MG8_proposal.md), the [MG036 response](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/3f88b8562f430419ca9a4c50731b28dd4063b74a/review/MG036_response.md), stage log, and relevant implementation at commit `3f88b856`. No files were changed.
The main corrections are sound: Q5 remains open; fate, shape, and drift have separate labels; stochastic results are descriptive; and the unsupported claim that R bounds other orders is withdrawn. I also independently summed A4’s corrected displacement distribution at \(r=0.2\): total probability is 1, mean is zero, variance is \(0.5h^2\), and third moment is \(0.375h^3\), agreeing with the revised formulas.

1. [High] Define what “deterministic R” means when the seam is random.
R draws a fresh seam every step. Replacing token draws with expected exits removes token noise, but does not remove scheduler randomness. As written, B1 is an expected-count trajectory conditional on one random seam sequence. Its fitted convergence slope can therefore depend on that sequence.
Distinguish three objects explicitly:
   * The map for a specified seam.
   * The seam-averaged one-step map.
   * A trajectory using randomly selected seams.
Choose which B1 tests, specify its seed or averaging procedure, and state how seam sequences relate across refinement levels. A fixed sequence is reproducible but is still one scheduler realization. The seam-averaged diffusion operator in Part 0 is not automatically the same experiment as production R. [§§3.1–3.2, 5.1](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/3f88b8562f430419ca9a4c50731b28dd4063b74a/spec/MG8_proposal.md)
2. [High] Correct two classification loopholes around the numerical floor.
SHAPE is NEGLIGIBLE if either \(E<10^{-3}\) or \(E<10E_{\rm floor}\). A large floor could therefore classify a material discrepancy as negligible—and overlap the MATERIAL rule. A large floor means insufficient resolution.
Likewise, MG8-C branch (b) can declare CONVERGES TO J when discrepancies merely fall below the floor. It has no explicit absolute-smallness requirement and bypasses the longer-horizon check imposed by branch (a).
Recommended correction: make inadequate resolution a separate outcome; make the shape categories mutually exclusive; and label floor-limited ladders “below measurement resolution” rather than demonstrated convergence. Apply the horizon safeguard to both branches. Specify the common-time J reference for the \(T=600\) comparison. [§§3.3, 4.2–4.3](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/3f88b8562f430419ca9a4c50731b28dd4063b74a/spec/MG8_proposal.md)
3. [High] Make Part 0’s operator benchmark internally consistent and informative.
The benchmark compares mean maps with seam/permutation-averaged operators to \(10^{-12}\), but production maps use particular orders. A fixed-order map generally differs from its order average. The proposal also says deterministic P is undefined, then requires its deterministic version in Part 0.
Define a deterministic conditional-order map for every order, and average those maps explicitly for the small-system benchmark.
Also, starting with equal counts at every node cannot validate the full averaged transition operator: a uniform field does not probe its nonconstant modes. Use a localized initial population or basis-vector initial conditions. A ring transition matrix records wrapped destinations; unwrapped displacement moments additionally require crossing or displacement information. Specify that augmented benchmark.
Finally, freeze the elapsed time, matrix norm, and time-step construction for the operator-refinement check. [§5.1](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/3f88b8562f430419ca9a4c50731b28dd4063b74a/spec/MG8_proposal.md)
4. [Medium] Finish specifying stochastic comparisons and uncertainty.
A shared seed does not provide controlled eventwise coupling, but that does not make the resulting arms independent. Bootstrap resampling should preserve replicate pairing and resample J alongside R/P, or the arms should use distinct independent streams.
The drift equivalence criterion currently describes an arm’s mean drift. Equivalence to J requires an interval for the difference \(v_{\rm arm}-v_J\).
Specify which runs contribute to stochastic shape estimates, how runs with lost or multiplied particles are handled, the minimum eligible count, and whether block profiles are weighted by their snapshot counts. The existing analysis routine excludes disqualified runs and returns block means with potentially unequal counts. Report shape comparisons as conditional on eligibility when applicable. [Proposal §§3.3, 4.4](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/3f88b8562f430419ca9a4c50731b28dd4063b74a/spec/MG8_proposal.md), [analysis routine](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/3f88b8562f430419ca9a4c50731b28dd4063b74a/src/measure.py)
5. [Medium] Store the inputs needed to reproduce the new decisions.
E′ uses all three species at \(T-50\), but the explicit storage schedule promises final full fields and intermediate smoothed X fields. That does not clearly preserve the G/Y inputs needed to independently reconstruct E′.
Similarly, velocities use unit-time measurements, whereas intermediate B1 X fields are saved every ten units. Store the unit-time centres and the full three-species fields at every shape/settledness checkpoint. Include reference arrays, seam seeds or schedules, alignment settings, and bootstrap settings. [§7](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/3f88b8562f430419ca9a4c50731b28dd4063b74a/spec/MG8_proposal.md)
6. [Medium] Narrow the remaining analytical claims in A4.
The third moment gives the stated leading third-derivative contribution; it is not the only parity-breaking term of the exact diffusion map, which also has higher odd moments. Mixed reaction–diffusion effects can contribute to particle motion as well.
The “roughly 20%” R4 re-exposure estimate is heuristic: hopping competes with reaction exits, and the local X-dependent rate changes during the sweep. Label it accordingly.
Finally, the slope’s “95% t interval” is a regression diagnostic unless a justified residual probability model is supplied. It should not be presented as calibrated uncertainty about the convergence order. [A4, §4.3](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/3f88b8562f430419ca9a4c50731b28dd4063b74a/spec/MG8_proposal.md)

Disposition: retain the revised scope, arm structure, and budget. Resolve items 1–3 before registration, and freeze items 4–6 in the specification before implementation review. No larger production study is needed to address these issues.
