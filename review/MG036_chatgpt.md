Recommendation: revise before registration. MG8 poses a useful numerical question, but Draft 1 contains an incorrect sweep-drift argument and draws conclusions about preferred frames that its experiments cannot establish.
I reviewed the connected repository at commit `0292b878`, including the MG8 proposal, update kernel, tracking and seed utilities, rule specification, gate record, stage log, and MG9d context. MG8 is Draft 1, unregistered, with no runs reported. I changed no files.
Objectives and sound elements
The proposed study asks two distinct questions: whether node-processing order changes particle behavior at the production time step, and whether those differences shrink under time-step refinement. J as the existing synchronous control, R/L as directional mirror checks, and P as a random-order comparison form a sensible starting design. Frozen criteria, a timing stage, explicit unresolved outcomes, and independent recomputation are also strengths. [MG8 proposal, §§1–7](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/0292b87883c9b630c1facfe3bf0db764b4a0ec17/spec/MG8_proposal.md)
The continuum argument A1 is substantially sound: these reaction–diffusion equations already distinguish the medium’s rest frame. That makes it especially important to distinguish scheduler sensitivity from evidence about the origin of a physical preferred frame.
The following findings are prioritized.

1. Blocking: A4’s predicted sweep advection is incorrect for the stated symmetric hopping rule.
The calculation counts repeated forward hops but omits backward hops during subsequent processing. With reactions disabled, processing a node holding \(n\) tokens sends an expected \(nr\) left and \(nr\) right. Its expected contribution to unwrapped displacement is therefore
\[ (-h)nr+(h)nr=0. \]
This remains true when that node contains arrivals from earlier processing. Summing these conditional expectations over the sweep gives zero expected net displacement, provided periodic crossings are unwrapped correctly.
Consequently, the claimed \(v_s=h r^2/[(1-r)\tau]\), Y velocity of 15, and derived Péclet estimates do not follow from the proposed scheduler. The registered kernel confirms equal left/right exit probabilities. This does not rule out changes to spreading, asymmetric profiles, nonlinear particle motion, or reaction–diffusion coupling; those require a different analysis. [Proposal A4 and Part 0](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/0292b87883c9b630c1facfe3bf0db764b4a0ec17/spec/MG8_proposal.md), [hopping kernel](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/0292b87883c9b630c1facfe3bf0db764b4a0ec17/src/lattice.py)
Required correction: replace A4 and its 5% validation target with a derivation of the actual diffusion-only sweep map. Validate conservation, zero expected unwrapped displacement, spreading, and convergence toward the synchronous limit.
2. Blocking: the outcome-to-Q5 mapping overstates what is identifiable.
B1 refines deterministic expected-count maps; B2 compares finite-\(\Omega\) stochastic runs only at \(\tau_0\). These do not establish stochastic scheduler equivalence as \(\tau\to0\). Conversely, a plateau over a finite ladder could reflect slow relaxation, detector error, mesh effects, or a scheme defect rather than a surviving physical frame.
Comparing node sweeps also does not directly test coordinate-frame transformations or causal-graph invariance. The proposal explicitly excludes the latter. Its claim that R bounds all “milder” orders needs a proof and should otherwise be removed. [Proposal §§3, 4, 8](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/0292b87883c9b630c1facfe3bf0db764b4a0ec17/spec/MG8_proposal.md)
Required correction: make the primary conclusion “scheduler sensitivity of this lattice approximation.” Report deterministic refinement separately. Keep the broader Q5 hypothesis open unless a further mathematical argument connects these measurements to it.
3. High priority: MG8-F can miss the shape effect it explicitly intends to detect.
Deterministic shape distortion alone can satisfy the first half of ORDER-DEPENDENT. But stochastic agreement requires either changed fate or consistently signed velocity. A persistent single particle with a distorted shape and zero drift can therefore produce UNRESOLVED even when deterministic and stochastic behavior agree.
Likewise, the proposed ORDER-INDEPENDENT stochastic criteria—similar fate counts and mean velocity within two standard errors—are not equivalence tests. Eight runs may simply be imprecise. [Proposal §4](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/0292b87883c9b630c1facfe3bf0db764b4a0ec17/spec/MG8_proposal.md)
Required correction: give fate, drift, and shape separate outcomes. Add a stochastic shape observable if it is confirmatory, or make stochastic findings descriptive. Define practical equivalence margins and use uncertainty intervals to distinguish equivalence from insufficient precision.
4. High priority: MG8-C needs stronger convergence safeguards.
Fitting four selected single-and-settled levels is reasonable exploratory analysis, but the current rules lack a numerical error floor, slope uncertainty, and a requirement that the chosen levels lie in an asymptotic regime. Near-zero \(E\) makes logarithms and relative settledness unstable. A fitted \(p\le0.2\) alone does not establish a nonzero limiting effect.
Shape convergence also does not establish velocity convergence: velocity is merely descriptive under the current rule. Earlier MG9d records explicitly caution that finite-horizon mesh-sensitive motion does not establish eventual pinning or a continuum limit. [Proposal §§3.3–4](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/0292b87883c9b630c1facfe3bf0db764b4a0ec17/spec/MG8_proposal.md), [MG9d interpretation](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/0292b87883c9b630c1facfe3bf0db764b4a0ec17/GATES.md)
Required correction: specify absolute-plus-relative settledness tolerances, a noise floor, minimum refinement span, fit diagnostics, and a bounded longer-horizon check. Label apparent plateaus “not resolved by this ladder” unless a positive limiting discrepancy is supported.
5. High priority: scheduler and random-stream semantics need an executable specification.
The draft says seeds are shared across arms but also uses `seed_for(8, 0, arm, 0, rep)`. The actual utility includes `arm` in the stream key, producing different seeds. Even identical seeds do not give eventwise coupling when schedulers consume random numbers differently.
Specify that all species’ exits at a node use one common pre-node-update snapshot, followed by simultaneous application of local changes. Otherwise species processing introduces an additional order effect. Define seam/permutation streams separately and state how mirrored random draws achieve exact R/L agreement. [Proposal §3](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/0292b87883c9b630c1facfe3bf0db764b4a0ec17/spec/MG8_proposal.md), [seed utility](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/0292b87883c9b630c1facfe3bf0db764b4a0ec17/src/runner.py)
6. Medium priority: narrow A2 and improve measurement auditability.
Additive count changes establish a commuting diamond for a particular pair that remains enabled. They do not establish global confluence. Moreover, event order changes later propensities even away from low-count resource conflicts. The count-\(\le2\) diagnostic measures proximity to disabling conditions, not stochastic order sensitivity generally. [Rule propensities](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/0292b87883c9b630c1facfe3bf0db764b4a0ec17/spec/rules_v1.1.md)
Also resolve whether fate means the first transient departure from one detection or MG2’s persistence-qualified classification. Validate tracking with synthetic moving, wrapping, disappearing, and splitting profiles. The existing tracker has a finite linking distance, so failed tracking must remain distinct from physical loss. [Tracking implementation](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/0292b87883c9b630c1facfe3bf0db764b4a0ec17/src/measure.py)

Validation required before production
I would retain the basic arm structure, subject to these prerequisites:

* An exact small-system diffusion benchmark replacing A4.
* Defined local-update and random-stream semantics, with J regression and R/L mirror checks.
* Probability-sum and clipping diagnostics for every scheduler; the existing kernel rescales excessive probabilities, which can change the dynamics.
* Synthetic validation of shape alignment, velocity, tracking, fate classification, and convergence labels.
* Committed detector inputs or sufficient snapshots for independent fate and shape recomputation. Hashes of PC-only fields establish integrity but do not enable independent reanalysis.
* Fresh timing of the sequential implementation, including random seams/permutations. Specify budget-stop labels and whether Part 0 counts toward the ceiling. [Kernel clipping](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/0292b87883c9b630c1facfe3bf0db764b4a0ec17/src/lattice.py), [proposed validation and budget](https://github.com/RichardBenear/ModelG/blob/0292b87883c9b630c1facfe3bf0db764b4a0ec17/spec/MG8_proposal.md)

The strongest next version would preserve MG8 as a bounded scheduler study, correct the diffusion argument, and separate measured order effects from claims about physical frames. Those changes are needed before registration; increasing the run count alone would not resolve the central problems.
