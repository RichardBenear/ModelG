# Prompt for Claude: amplitude-weighted center diagnostic

Please implement a short, reproducible **post-result diagnostic of MG030/MG9c**, using the committed snapshots and tracks. The question is whether the reported drift reflects coherent translation, amplitude redistribution, or movement of the envelope threshold crossings. Do not change MG030's classifications or run new integrations for this analysis.

1. Analyze the three primary D02 runs (b = 29.25, 29.30, 29.35); include 29.22 descriptively. At every saved field time, compute the boundary midpoint `C_edge = (x_L + x_R)/2` and the amplitude-weighted center `C_amp = sum(x * E**2) / sum(E**2)`, where E is the frozen MG027 envelope of phi_X. Use the full domain, without a moving threshold mask. Use a consistent unwrapped coordinate branch with the periodic cut in the background; document the convention. Also compute the phi_X**2-weighted center as a sensitivity check, and report each weight's total and weighted spatial variance. These are amplitude diagnostics, not energy, mass or information densities; their totals need not be conserved.

2. Compare **changes** in centers from the first saved snapshot, not just absolute offsets. Report the late interval [15000,30000] separately. Track identifiable interior minima with sub-grid localization to assess whether they shift together. Independently estimate whole-profile translation by aligning successive phi_X snapshots, reporting the remaining normalized shape difference. Resolve periodic/cell-spacing ambiguities by continuity; state the alignment convention and flag ambiguous matches.

3. Validate on synthetic patches before interpreting results: known sub-grid translation, symmetric amplitude relaxation without displacement, and asymmetric amplitude changes with fixed peak positions. The last case should demonstrate that a weighted centroid itself can move through redistribution. Quantify measurement error relative to the approximately 0.008-unit late drift.

Deliver a compact table, a plot of center changes versus time, and reproducible code/results following repository conventions. State whether the diagnostics agree, disagree or remain inconclusive. Agreement supports coherent translation but does not identify its cause; do not infer a mesh effect from this diagnostic alone. Disclose the sparse snapshot times and label this analysis exploratory. Any further simulations or new pass/fail criteria should be proposed separately.
