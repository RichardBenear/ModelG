## Project purpose

E1 established a seeded field response to the location of A supply. E2 asks whether restoring that supply changes the response compared with keeping it interrupted, and whether the response depends on seed history. Fixed-coordinate and movable-template measurements distinguish some translation and shape changes. Recovery is an observable change, not proof that the same particle survived; a newly formed structure influenced by residual seed information is not distinguishable as physical lineage. Classical history dependence is not quantum memory or entanglement.
