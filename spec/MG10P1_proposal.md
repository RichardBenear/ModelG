## Why this precedes interventions

D1/D2 measured means and spatial contrast. Those do not establish localization or persistent identity. Before calling supply-induced changes particle death/replication, qualify an instrument on controlled inputs whose field content is known. Even a good field tracker cannot distinguish an unobserved replacement by an identical pattern at the same location. This study measures only sampled continuity of a single isolated reference-like X motif. Multiple/merged patterns, gaps and ambiguous movement censor continuity; they are not counted as losses or assigned new identities. Multi-particle lineage remains outside scope.
