## Purpose, model boundary and fixed choices

G1 asks whether fixed environmental connectivity changes where/when resources reach unchanged contacts even after matching node/edge counts, per-node transport capacity and the direct source-to-contact hop distance. This is the first controlled geometry-supply test in the constructed network model, using simple resource probes and the N1 exact SSA. It is not yet a particle-on-network experiment, native-vacuum derivation or entanglement/energy/spacetime-curvature test. Geometry here means abstract graph connectivity. No Euclidean edge-length factor or graph growth is introduced. A/B stocks and contacts remain postulated; this tests a mechanism linking their effective delivery to connectivity, not their physical origin.

Two6-node connected graphs: triangular prism (two triangles and three matching links) versus complete bipartite K3,3. Both9undirected unit-weight edges/18directed, all nodes degree3, equal-volume nodes, same per-edge kappa=1/3 for both resources, total outgoing environmental hazard1per token at every node. Source fixednode0/contact fixednode3, direct link0-3 in both, so distance1. Nonisomorphic (triangle counts2vs0). Source/contact identity map to same six active sites. Active hopping disabled in these probes, all other reaction channels disabled except specified below; existing active lattice not rewired. This isolates environmental delivery rather than nonlinear Model G particle feedback. No replenishment, recycling or stock reset; all seven species and directed transfer counters retained, closed total counts exact.

Four resource/topology cells. A probe: one A at0, no other counts; uniform fixed A0=28,Omega1 gives A->G hazard.5per A at every node, G inert. Local delivery is cumulative F1at contact3, equivalently G3. All six A contacts are identical across graphs; conversion elsewhere is competing loss for delivery to3. Total conversion probability1-exp(-.5t) is exactly graph-independent, a structural null control. B probe: one B at0, one locked X at3; fixed B0=36.25,Omega1, only B+X->Y+Z enabled, so target capture hazard.8 when B at3; X/B consumed and Y/Z produced. No other stock; total2tokens, not catalytic B. Source references are deliberately underfilled as N1 fixtures, not renormalized to current counts. A/B comparisons are within the same resource mechanism across topologies, never a direct claim A and B are interchangeable.

All resource contacts/reference stocks/initials/rates/active hopping masks/timepoints identical across topologies. Four cells differ only resource mechanism and graph, no random geometry or outcome selection. Existing frozen N1 exact clock/updates and stream functions reused without modification. Graph adjacency adds no new rule. Source/target labelling is fixed, not fitted to observed trajectories.
