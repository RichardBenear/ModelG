## Purpose and deliberately limited scope

E1/E2 established local-resource and restoration responses within imposed supplies. N1 builds and qualifies a fixed environmental transport/contact model: resource delivery depends on a connected stock network instead of independent baths. This is a formal, deterministic and exact-small-system stochastic qualification, not a geometry/particle survival experiment, native vacuum derivation, energy identification or entanglement claim. Full nonlinear Model G fast-mixing recovery and practical full-lattice stochastic acceleration are not established by linear limits/small exact SSA. No geometry evolves.

One equal-volume environmental node per active lattice node, identity contact map. Active G/X/Y remain on the existing periodic lattice; A/B move only on a separate fixed symmetric weighted graph. Z/W remain inert local output stores. Undirected positive weighted edges become two directed edges, no self/duplicate edges. Symmetry/equal volume is explicit; directed or unequal-volume networks require separate specification. Stock references A0/B0 remain fixed initially specified positive denominators, not renormalized as resources deplete. Deliberately depleted/one-token test fixtures need not initially fill these reference stocks: A0/B0 fix the contact coefficients, not the actual instantaneous populations. Existing finite Model G reactions and active hopping retained. New events A_i->A_j with propensity kappa_A*w_ij*A_i and B_i->B_j with kappa_B*w_ij*B_i. kappa has inverse-model-time units; graph weights dimensionless, no automatic lattice h^-2 factor. B+X consumes one B and one X, producing Y+Z, never treats B as catalyst. No injectors, chemostats, exports, recycling or silent stock reset. Closed seven-species total conserved; cumulative directed graph counts per species reconstruct every change alongside reaction/active-hop counts. Token-count conservation is not energy conservation.

Exact Gillespie SSA handles shared-reactant competition one event at a time on tiny systems. All events occur at their continuous times; this does not resolve the fundamental-tick hypothesis. No tau-leap transport approximation introduced. Disconnected/zero-kappa SSA explicitly delegates the qualified MG10-S1 reaction/active-hop clock and reuses update semantics, requiring bit-identical legacy data/stats on4small fixtures. This regression is not an independent oracle. Connected networks use directly assembled rates; independent Python SSA uses separate RNG/event lists/explicit updates, never calls new clock or stoichiometry helper. Nonfinite/negative counts/rates/graph inputs reject; total count<=2^53 and per-trajectory event cap1000000 prevent unbounded qualified domain. Failed/budget paths stop batch, never replace or score as physical losses.

## Formal/deterministic limits

1. Integer event accounting, nonnegative populations, closed total and disconnected-component environment boundaries (active transport can still cross lattice sites).
2. No environmental hopping: same frozen finite reaction/active-hop laws, bitwise regression on four small fixtures.
3. Mean-field concentration equations: existing finite Model G drift plus graph Laplacian on A/B. Finite-count R4 has X(X-1)Y/Omega^2; continuum has x^2*y. These are distinct and are not silently substituted. Pointwise zero-kappa continuum RHS must match old mg10_reservoir.ode_rhs via uA/uB conversion at a declared positive nonuniform state; local normalized activities1 recover old maintained active drift at that instant. No finite-horizon nonlinear-particle convergence conclusion.
4. Transport-only matrix exponential reference: symmetric graph approaches uniform stock within each connected component. Disconnected component test via block generator must have exactly no cross-component flow within numerical tolerance1e-12.
5. Fast-mixing linear A-source/absorbed-G reference,3-node weighted path, heterogeneous contact rates14/A0 with A0=(20,28,35), initial one A at0. At T1,kappa=(1,10,100,1000), compare exact6-state matrix exponential to well-mixed bath with averaged contact hazard and rate-weighted G production. Require strictly decreasing infinity-norm difference and final<.001. This is a linear contact limit, not rapid-mixing proof for full nonlinear network.
6. Large-capacity linear source limit: uniform initial A0=14*C, zero other reactions, cumulative source14*C*(-expm1(-T/C)) approaches imposed14*T atT1, C=(1,100,10000). Errors strictly decrease and final relative error<.0001. Derivation/series checked separately; not full Model G finite-capacity equivalence.
