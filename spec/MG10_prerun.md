## Representation and numerical methods

Deterministic initial stocks use the Omega->infinity limits, without count rounding:
A0=C*14*300 and B0=C*29*(140/9)*300 concentration units at each node.
No Omega or individual bath-token loop is introduced.

Saved state has shape (time,15,500), float64, field-major ordering:
G,X,Y,uA,uB,F1,F2,F2r,F3,F4,F5,F1r,HG,HX,HY.
uA/uB are depleted fractions, availability is 1-uA/1-uB. This avoids subtraction
of tiny withdrawals from enormous stocks and cancellation in accounting checks.
F are cumulative positive reaction fluxes in concentration units; F1r is exactly
zero at P17. Z=F3 and W=F5, so these seven species are fully reconstructible.
HS is cumulative total outgoing hopping for species S. Net incoming-minus-outgoing
is (HS_left+HS_right)/2-HS. Incoming plus outgoing determines absolute throughput.
CHEMOSTAT replenishment is F1-F1r for A and F3 for B; FINITE has zero external input.
No ledger residual is normalized by initial stock.

Euler updates all fields from the same active-state snapshot, with no clipping.
The independent vectorized ODE RHS does not call the Euler increment. Its analytic
Jacobian is assembled as sparse COO entries and converted to CSC; periodic active
diffusion and all local derivatives are included, and directional finite differences
test it. BDF uses the MG040 rtol/atol/max_step settings and dense interpolation at
exact integer checkpoints. Identically constant coordinates are eliminated before
integration: F1r for all modes, plus uA/uB for CHEMOSTAT. Reconstructed zeros are
structural constants, not post hoc clipping of integrated negative values.

## Development qualification and forecast

Tests cover stoichiometry, per-channel ledger examples, zero supply, nonuniform and
homogeneous CHEMOSTAT/IDEAL agreement, all exact Euler steps, Jacobian, mirror,
stock accounting, exact availability, unchanged IDEAL stochastic regression and
invalid/incomplete/timeout/budget decision records. Only development seed key
(10,99,0,0,0), entropy 20261027, is used by the unchanged stochastic regression.
There is no finite stochastic sampler or stochastic production in this batch.

An initial development test revealed sparse-solver roundoff at about 1e-30 in
identically zero chemostat depletion coordinates. Constant-coordinate elimination
corrected it; the failed test record is preserved. No tolerance was relaxed.

The two declared timing trajectories are FINITE C=1, soliton start, T=10:
E2 and O. They do not enter recovery outcomes. Use twice the measured linear
extrapolation to T=300, Euler step-count scaling, and five seconds/job overhead.
Use this conservative bound for every mode/start/capacity class; no capacity factor.
The full 64-run projection must fit 75% of 14400 worker-seconds and each projected
job must fit 600 seconds. Qualification/timing costs include failed attempts.

## Dispatch and retention

The fixed matrix is generated by run_mg10.jobs(). Never retry or replace an outcome.
Use <=8 owned subprocesses, library threads=1. A half-second parent poll includes
in-flight elapsed times; global ceiling stops all owned jobs, timeout stops the
affected owned job. Completed and interrupted jobs remain in the ledger; missing
jobs keep their denominator. Recheck remaining forecast before dispatch waves.
A per-directory exclusive lock prevents concurrent drivers. Run-level resume reads
the ledger and never silently reruns a recorded failed or interrupted job.

Workers atomically retain checkpoints every 25 physical time units and at the final
time. Partial files survive interruption. Successful raw files contain every integer
checkpoint, all fields and cumulative fluxes, plus metadata with source provenance
provided by the committed registration. Each job's full file is below 100 MiB.

The durable primary store is results/MG10/production in this isolated local Windows
ModelG checkout, not temporary cloud storage. Verify write/read and available disk
space before registration. Commit all compressed decision and ledger arrays; GitHub
will provide a second retained copy. SHA-256 verifies every uploaded local raw file.
The prior retention wording 'PI ModelG working area' includes this isolated working
checkout, avoiding modifications to the PI's potentially dirty Documents checkout.

The evaluator and separate checker read raw arrays independently. The checker does
not import the evaluator or model implementation. Both deliberately share the frozen
MG2 detector/smoothing and reference helpers; declare that limit. Same-author checking
is not independent external replication. Primary errors stay unaligned; alignment and
background removal are descriptive only. PI approval is required for gate closure.
