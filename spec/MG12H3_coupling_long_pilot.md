# MG157 — full-horizon paired variance pilot

PI authorization: “go ahead and do the longer test.” This approves executing the next longer pilot, not a final physical or tick-equivalence conclusion. MG156 closure remains separate. Freeze source, seeds, criteria and this specification before draws; publish before launching. Use the frozen MG156 joint sampler and frozen sparse marginal engine without edits.

## Purpose and design

Test whether the small-horizon reduction of Y tick-difference noise survives to T=0.01, 100 times the previous horizon. Canonical finite LOCAL/LOCAL Omega=10, 500-site state; tick comparisons (0.0001,0.00002) and (0.00005,0.00002). Eight independent coupled pairs and eight independent reference pairs per comparison: 16 coupled jobs plus 16 jobs each containing two independent chains. Fresh, disjoint seed leaves. All chains start at the same frozen state. Observe every 0.001 through 0.01. Compartments, rates, masks, strict reservations and physical interpretation unchanged. Use direct fixed unrounded-template G/X/Y contrasts only; no fitted weights or compensator/blend in the new joint engine.

Continuing at common checkpoint boundaries with the same random-generator state gives the same Markov law as a whole trajectory. At these boundaries each chain resets availability anyway. Each 0.001 segment is a separate frozen pair invocation; the RNG continues, not reseeds. Keep each side's own tick calendar inside a segment, including nonnested calendars. One million joint-event cap per segment; per-job deadline 1190 seconds inside a 1200-second controller cap.

## Prerequisite controls

Separate 120-second, one-worker/thread-one development allowance. One-site five-token A,B,X,X,Y fixture, Omega=2, references A0=10/B0=4, all six reactions enabled, no transport. Independently construct exact one-tick identity-level augmented generators for all reachable count states, compose the resulting kernels at ticks 0.01 and 0.004 through 0.02 and 0.04. Retain kernels, support, probabilities and all samples. Compare 8192 fresh coupled pairs with each of the four exact marginal/time laws: observed TV <= sqrt((S ln2 + ln(4/.001))/(2*8192)). This is a conservative null-consistency screen, not TV<0.10 qualification. Sixty-four separate paths compare whole versus segmented execution including final RNG state; replay full small-system traces. Any control failure blocks all longer jobs. No automatic retries, sample replacement or threshold changes.

## Outcome and analysis

All 32 jobs must complete, source hashes remain fixed, counts remain nonnegative and globally conserved, and all saved checkpoints reconstruct from channel histograms. Replay every retained paired sentinel event and reservation boundary through the separate reconstruction path before reporting.

Primary time is T=0.01; primary species Y. For each comparison compute variance of coupled differences divided by variance of independent differences, then multiply by mean elapsed cost per coupled job divided by mean elapsed cost per independent-pair job. Costs include checkpoint/trace writing within each worker. Eight pairs make this an estimation pilot, not a definitive variance qualification. Freeze 10000 ordinary bootstrap resamples: sample eight coupled replicate indices and eight independent replicate indices independently, preserving the left/right and all-species vectors within each replicate; resample their corresponding elapsed costs as well. Use the 97.5th percentile upper bound for each of two primary cost-adjusted ratios. Report “pilot benefit supported” only if both upper bounds are below one and both unadjusted point ratios are below one; otherwise “not established.” The two upper bounds give a nominal Bonferroni adjustment, but bootstrap coverage at n=8 is approximate, not a finite-sample guarantee. Preserve degenerate resamples; undefined primary ratios make that comparison unresolved, rather than discarding draws. Report raw ratios, cost ratios and bootstrap bounds individually. G/X and intermediate observations are descriptive secondary outputs. No post-hoc estimator changes.

Do not declare tick equivalence, 5% contrast accuracy, particle persistence or a quantum/vacuum interpretation from this pilot. Genuine differences in tick laws remain in the mean differences. Report paired means and their ordinary standard errors, but reserve any formal equivalence decision for its own prospective study.
